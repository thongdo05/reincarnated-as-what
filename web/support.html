Support

Reincarnated as WHAT?! is a landscape game. One life. Drag to move. The ship fires on its own. It is on iOS, Android, GitHub Pages, and CrazyGames.

Privacy: what the game stores.

Restore Remove Ads

Remove Ads exists on the iOS and Android apps. Apple or Google Play takes the payment. The GitHub Pages build and the CrazyGames build do not sell it.

On a phone: pause the game, then tap Restore. The game asks the store whether this Apple or Google account already owns Remove Ads. If it does, the death-card banner and the full-screen ad between runs stop, and choosing the next form no longer asks for an ad.

FAQ

The game plays the same if I decline tracking. On iOS the first launch can show Apple's tracking prompt. Choosing Ask App Not to Track does not change how the game plays.

I skipped the rewarded ad. The next form stays the normal roll. The picker opens only after the ad grants the reward.

Where is my leaderboard name stored? The name you type, a random device id, and the score are sent to the online leaderboard if you post a run. Best scores and the sound setting also stay on the device. On CrazyGames those device saves go through the CrazyGames SDK.

Can I delete a leaderboard row? There is no account and no delete button. Email the address below with the leaderboard name.
