Privacy policy

This policy covers Reincarnated as WHAT?! on iOS, Android, GitHub Pages, and CrazyGames. The game does not create an account. There is no login.

Leaderboard

If you post a score, the game sends these fields to a Supabase database:

  • the leaderboard name you type
  • a random id the game creates and stores on the device, so that device keeps its row. It is not the advertising identifier
  • the score, how long the run lasted, which form it was, and whether that run defeated the dragon

The game does not send your email, phone number, contacts, photos, precise location, or health data. It does not sell this data.

There is no delete button in the game. To ask us to delete the leaderboard row for a name, email playreincarnated@gmail.com and include that name.

Saved on the device

Best scores, the daily-run index, the leaderboard name and device id, and the sound on/off setting are stored on the device. The iOS, Android, and GitHub Pages builds keep those in local save files.

On iOS and Android the game also stores, on the device only, whether Remove Ads was bought and how many runs have finished. It does not send the receipt or the payment to us.

The CrazyGames build stores best scores, the daily-run index, the leaderboard name and device id, and the sound setting through the CrazyGames SDK Data module after the SDK starts. If an older local save is already in that browser, it is copied into the module once. CrazyGames syncs that module for players who are logged in to CrazyGames. The game also loads the CrazyGames SDK, which reports gameplay events to CrazyGames. This page does not describe what CrazyGames itself collects.

Ads on iOS and Android

The iOS and Android apps use Google AdMob. The GitHub Pages build and the CrazyGames build do not load AdMob and do not sell Remove Ads.

On the phone apps, ads are:

  • a banner on the death card, hidden during play and after Remove Ads
  • a full-screen ad between runs, not during the first five completed runs, then after every five completed runs, and not during a boss fight
  • an optional rewarded ad on the death card if you want to choose the next form. Skipping it or a failed ad leaves the next form as the normal roll

Before ads start, the phone app can show Google's ads consent form when Google says that form is required.

On iOS, the first launch can show Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt: "This lets Reincarnated as WHAT?! show ads that are more relevant to you between runs. The game plays the same if you choose Ask App Not to Track." The game asks for that permission and then starts ads either way. It does not read the advertising identifier itself. If you allow tracking, AdMob can use it. If you choose Ask App Not to Track, iOS does not give AdMob that identifier.

Remove Ads

On iOS and Android you can buy Remove Ads. Apple or Google Play handles the payment and shows the price. The product id in the game is remove_ads. After the store says the purchase is owned, the banner and the full-screen ad stop, and choosing the next form no longer asks for an ad. The game keeps that fact on the device. It does not send the receipt to us.

Pause, then tap Restore, to ask Apple or Google Play again. If the store says you own Remove Ads, the ads stop. If the store says you do not, the ads can come back.
